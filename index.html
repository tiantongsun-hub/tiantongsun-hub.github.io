Research

Fairness + robustness

FUSE: Fairness-aware Update Screening against Byzantine Exploitation in q-Fair Federated Learning

Tiantong Sun, Jinlin Liu, Xingru Wei, Zequan Fang, Wei Chen, Qiang Yang, Xiaojin Zhang
First author · Preprint, 2026 · Under review

Fairness mechanisms can amplify honest updates, making them look suspicious to a defense. I study how collaborative learning can distinguish malicious contributions without suppressing the participants that fairness is intended to protect.

Selective sharing + privacy

FedPix: Learning What to Share with Less Leakage in Federated Intelligence

Jingwei Zhao*, Tiantong Sun*, Yuanyuan Song, Jinhuan Guo, Zishuo Wei, Shengyu Huang, Ziyuan Ren, Wei Chen, Xiaojin Zhang
* Equal contribution · Preprint, 2026 · Under review

What information should leave a client? We investigate sharing compact, visually inspectable representations that retain task-relevant information while limiting reconstruction. The privacy evaluation is empirical; it is not a formal privacy guarantee.

Learning behavior + data protection

From Unlearnable Outcomes to Decodable Shortcut Codes

Research project · Second author · Under review

Low clean accuracy does not tell us what a model has learned. This project examines when perturbations act as decodable shortcuts, connecting data protection to the information that learners actually use.

Ongoing research

Personalized collaboration among heterogeneous agents

I am exploring when experience should transfer across agents with different users, tasks, and capabilities. My goal is to connect robust collaboration with selective sharing, so that shared experience helps the particular learner receiving it.
